# Direction: Source Desk

The source desk is a dense post-execution workspace: changed paths, source or
receipt detail, selected finding, executable evidence, and limitations share a
single technical plane. It is stronger after evidence exists than as an empty
setup experience.

Its composition becomes the production receipt state after the chamber emits a
versioned Rust result.
