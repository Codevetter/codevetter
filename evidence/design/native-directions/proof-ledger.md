# Direction: Proof Ledger

The proof ledger presents identity, correctness, performance, and verdict as a
numbered evidence chain. It communicates CodeVetter's execution-first model
well, but a persistent left icon rail and stacked ledger make the setup state
feel more navigational and less decisive than the selected chamber.

Its evidence-chain model remains in the production proof inspector.

Rendered evidence: `artifacts/design/native-probes/ledger.png`.
